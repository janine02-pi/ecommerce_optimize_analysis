# 📊 E-commerce Profit Margin Optimization Analysis

## Overview

This project focuses on analyzing the operational and commercial performance of an E-commerce business through behavioral, transactional, and profitability data.

The analysis was conducted using over **472,000 website sessions** and **40,000+ cart/order records** to identify critical bottlenecks affecting:

* Profit Margin
* Conversion Rate (CR)
* Mobile User Experience (UX)
* Cross-selling effectiveness
* Average Order Value (AOV)

The project combines business analysis and data analytics techniques to transform raw operational data into actionable business recommendations.

---

# 🎯 Business Context

Although the company generated nearly **$1.93M in Gross Revenue**, profitability performance was significantly impacted by:

* High operational costs (COGS)
* Refund rates
* Mobile conversion inefficiencies
* Funnel drop-offs during checkout
* Underutilized cross-selling opportunities

The business also faced challenges related to:

* Fragmented marketing and financial data
* Delays in data synchronization pipelines (2–4 hours)
* Heavy dependence on Google Search traffic
* Poor mobile purchasing experience

The project aims to uncover the hidden factors behind margin erosion and propose data-driven strategies to optimize revenue quality rather than focusing solely on vanity metrics.

---

# 🚀 Project Objectives

The main objectives of this analysis are:

1. Analyze the real profitability structure behind gross revenue
2. Identify products with strong or weak net margin performance
3. Evaluate traffic quality and conversion performance across channels/devices
4. Detect bottlenecks in the purchasing funnel
5. Analyze cross-selling behavior to improve AOV and customer value
6. Generate strategic recommendations for Product, Marketing, and Business teams

# 🔍 Key Insights

## 1. Revenue Does Not Equal Profitability

Although the business generated nearly **$1.93M Gross Revenue**, hidden operational costs and refund rates significantly reduced actual net margins.

### Major Findings

* Gross Revenue acted as a vanity metric
* Refunds and COGS heavily impacted real profitability
* Margin analysis revealed large profitability gaps across products

### Product-Level Insight

* **Mr. Fuzzy** generated over **$1.2M revenue**, but its refund rate (~5.1%) and operational costs reduced net margin to only **55%**
* **Hudson River Mini Bear** showed strong profitability potential with a net margin of **67.28%**
